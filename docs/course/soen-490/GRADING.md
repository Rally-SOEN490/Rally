## Calculations

- **Poor / OK / Expected / Exceptional = 1 / 2 / 3 / 4.** Average the applicable indicator ratings within each component, then divide by 4 and multiply by its maximum points. Use the source scheme for assessment descriptions.
- Justify genuinely inapplicable indicators as **N/A**; exclude them from the average, redistributing weight within that component.
- Each student's release grade = **team points (maximum 85) + individual points (maximum 15)**, out of 100. Individual points = (peer-evaluation average ÷ 4 × 9) + (reflection rating ÷ 4 × 6).

## Individual reflection

Each student needs a reflection essay on knowledge/skill gaps encountered, their impact on the project, and a plan for further learning.

The reflection essay's length, submission route, and separate deadline remain unspecified. Do not invent these details.
