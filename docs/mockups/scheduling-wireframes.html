Low-fidelity layouts for match formats and the schedule. Phone and web side by side. Redraw in Figma with the app's components.

One format per pool and per playoff round (Release 2: one for pools, one for playoffs). The court time and the estimated end of the day update as the organizer types, so they see right away when sets are too long for the courts they have.
