1 · Build the schedule in the Schedule tab

Everything the schedule needs is already in the tournament, the Pools tab and the Playoffs tab, so the Schedule tab only adds two options and a Generate button. Generate fills a grid: courts across, time slots down, one day at a time, like a court scheduler but on a phone. Each cell shows the two teams and the referee; the left edge is the pool's colour. Pick a tool, then tap a match.

4 · The same grid on a computer

On a computer the grid has room for side panels: courts across, time slots down, one day at a time. Each cell is a match with its pool and referee. Tools: Move, Swap, Referee, Lock and Remove. Every change is checked against the rules right away; errors block Publish and warnings ask to confirm. "Generate again" keeps the locked matches.
