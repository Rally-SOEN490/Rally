Tournament schedule mockups (RALLY-257)

A new Schedule tab next to Results and the match tabs. The players see the published schedule; the organizer generates it, edits it and publishes it. The schedule shown was generated by the spike's prototype: 8 teams, 3 courts, 55' matches, teams referee.

F5.1 is the new tab in the same header as the scoreboard frames. A player sees their next match, every time slot grouped like the Match tabs, and the court and referee of each match. Their own matches are outlined and their refereeing duties are tagged. F5.1b shows only the player's team, and F5.1c the empty state before the organizer publishes.

After Generate, the organizer edits on a grid: courts across, time slots down, one tab per day. A cell shows the match and its referee. The tools work like the reference scheduler: move, swap, referee, lock and remove. Problems appear on the right after each change. Errors block Publish; the draft still saves.
